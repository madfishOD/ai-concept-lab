# Nocturna — provisional context, not approved art bible

Existing ING Unity renders are reference candidates, not art-lead-approved standards.
Stylized game character, simplified sculpted planes, readable full-body silhouette.
Gothic/vampiric costume language; violet, charcoal, ivory and controlled crimson accents.
Use broad material groups and moderate ornament density. Avoid photorealism, tiny filigree,
uniform silver piping, cinematic darkness, particles, backgrounds, and illustration clutter.
Do not copy setup poses, floating props or renderer artifacts from source previews.
Generate an original design rather than copying an existing roster character.
Human artist decides whether the result is useful for paintover and consistent with the game.

For masked edits describe the desired visible replacement, its material and attachment.
The strict mask is enforced by the application; do not claim the model guarantees it.
No animation, rigging, 3D generation, multi-view consistency or Photoshop automation in v0.1.
